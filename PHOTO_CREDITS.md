## Notes
- Check the license on each Commons page when you download; Commons pages are the authority, not this table.
- The two Wtshymanski files are marked public domain; I confirmed this from secondary sources, so verify on the file page.
- Two photos are only 640 × 480 (Creyke Beck, 115 kV breaker). They are fine at the page's column width but will look soft on large screens.
- The CC BY-SA photos require credit and a license link (already in each caption). If you modify a photo (crop, recolour), say so in the caption; ShareAlike then applies to your modified version.
- Large originals (several MB) slow the page. Resize to about 1600 px wide and save as JPEG before committing.
- The Air Force photo shows identifiable personnel; DoD rules ask for photographer credit and no implied endorsement.
